About these charts
CPM
Counts Per Minute from the Geiger–Müller tube. Each tick is one detected ionizing event. Typical natural background: 20–50 CPM.
CPM rolling mean
Time-windowed average of CPM (1-hour window). Smooths Poisson noise to reveal trends — independent of gmcmap's server-side ACPM, which uses a window we can't see.
Dose rate (µSv/h)
Equivalent dose rate in microsieverts per hour. Typical natural background outdoors: 0.05–0.15 µSv/h. EPA annual public dose limit ≈ 0.11 µSv/h continuous.
Radon (pCi/L)
Radon activity concentration in picocuries per liter (Radon detector only). WHO reference: 2.7 pCi/L. EPA action level for mitigation: 4 pCi/L.
Radon rolling mean
Same 1-hour rolling window applied to pCi/L.
Threshold lines
Dashed horizontal lines mark reference levels. Lines outside the chart's autoscaled range are pinned to the top of the chart so they stay visible.
